REFERENCE STACK

What “accurate” should mean.

Numerical verification needs a documented ephemeris plus fixed settings. Agreement between websites is useful evidence of consistency, but not proof of scientific validity for astrological predictions.

IMPORTANT

Calculation and prediction are different things.

Planetary positions can be reproduced numerically. Astrological predictions are interpretations and are not scientifically established facts.
